GroundTruth in two minutes

A walk through the live site, recorded from it: one day under Brussels, Ask the ground on Christmas Day, March 2020 city by city, the time-travel globe and the daily Today page.

The captions are part of the picture, and the same words are in the subtitle track. The sound is the site's own drone, made in the browser. Every number on screen comes from the site's own data files, built from Lecocq et al. (2020) and EarthScope's MUSTANG service.
