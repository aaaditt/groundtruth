158 seismometers, one question

Can a seismometer tell a weekday from a weekend? We trained one model per city on the published recordings of Lecocq et al. (2020), Oct 2019 – early 2020, and tested each on whole weeks it had never seen.

Weekday vs weekend accuracy, city by city

Each dot is a seismometer. Darker = the weekly rhythm is easier to read. Chance is 50%. Hover or tap a dot for its numbers.

Which frequencies carry the human rhythm?

The 7 live stations, Oct 2025 – Sep 2026, the same model on different frequency bands. Grey lines: individual stations. Blue: median. Red dashed line: chance. Hover a point for its city.

0.1–1 Hz is the ocean microseism (waves pounding coastlines). It has no weekly rhythm, which is exactly what should happen if the model is reading people. The 4–14 Hz band used by Lecocq et al. is the best overall. Dublin is the odd one out: something steady at 1–4 Hz hides its rhythm unless that band is excluded.

What else we found

  • 76% of weekday public holidays sounded like a weekend to a model that had never been told what a holiday is (vs 6% of ordinary weekdays). New Year 90%, Christmas/Easter 85%.
  • When the UAE moved its weekend on 1 Jan 2022, Sunday at the UAE station got 20% quieter (pre-registered test, p = 0.003; placebo year: no change).
  • A model trained only on Saturday–Sunday countries heard Friday as the weekend in Salfit (Palestine) and Amatzia (Israel).
  • Lockdown did not sound like a permanent Sunday: only 26% of lockdown weekdays were called "weekend". It was a new, flatter pattern, which an "unlike normal" detector flags more often than a weekday classifier does.
