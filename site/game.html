How to read it

  • Weekdays have a sharp morning rise (the commute) and a busy, flat working day.
  • Saturdays start later and lower.
  • Sundays are the quietest and latest of all.
  • Telling Tuesday from Wednesday is genuinely hard, for you and for the model.
  • Public holidays usually look like a Sunday. When they do, the model is "wrong" about the weekday but right about the city: people were behaving as if it were Sunday.

Real data from Lecocq et al. (2020), Science, doi:10.1126/science.abd2438, Oct 2019 – Mar 2020. The model's guesses are out-of-fold: it never saw any day from the same week.
